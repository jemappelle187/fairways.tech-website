## 🎯 LINK STRATEGY

### Why /about vs /impact?

**Banks, Buyers, Governments → `/about`**
- Need compliance, governance, regulatory detail
- Want to understand infrastructure + frameworks
- Technical/institutional focus

**Farmer groups, Development, Knowledge → `/impact`**
- Need outcomes, farmer results, impact evidence
- Want to see livelihood improvements + SDG metrics
- Human/social focus

**Result:** Each visitor lands on the page most relevant to their needs ✅
