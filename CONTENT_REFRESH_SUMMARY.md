### 3. "OUR SOLUTION" – Simplified Language

**Status:** ✅ Complete

**What Changed:**

```

**Main Paragraph (After):**
```

**Bullet Points (Before → After):**

| "Compliance-first rails that make risk visible for local financial institutions" | "**Bank-ready data** — Data that is structured, traceable, and aligned with AML/CFT and KYC standards, so institutions can plug it into their existing risk processes." |

**Impact:**
- ✅ Removed "fragmented farm activity" jargon
- ✅ Explained "what we capture" in simple terms
- ✅ Replaced "compliance-first rails" with "Bank-ready data"
- ✅ Added concrete explanations to each bullet
- ✅ Maintained technical credibility while improving accessibility
