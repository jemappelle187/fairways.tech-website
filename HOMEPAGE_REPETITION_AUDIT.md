## 🎯 RECOMMENDED CHANGES

### CHANGE 1: Trim "Our solution" Section (HIGH PRIORITY) ✂️

**BEFORE (2 paragraphs + 3 bullets):**
```
Fairways.Tech is a digital platform that helps smallholder farmers access finance from licensed banks and financial institutions.

We capture simple but powerful information — fields, crops, seasons, transactions, and repayments — through local agents and cooperatives. Licensed banks provide the liquidity, while our rails route funds directly to input suppliers and service providers. Farmers receive the support they need at the right moment, and banks see a clear, compliant flow of funds instead of cash leaking into the informal economy.

• Verified farmer profiles — Digital profiles that show who the farmer is, where they farm, what they grow, and how they perform over time.
• Farm-to-market tracking — A clear record of inputs, harvests, and sales so partners can see how crops move through the value chain.
• Bank-ready data — Data that is structured, traceable, and aligned with AML/CFT and KYC standards, so institutions can plug it into their existing risk processes.
```

**AFTER (1 short intro + 3 bullets):**
```
Digital infrastructure that turns farm activity into trusted records — so banks and partners can confidently finance inputs and services.

• Verified farmer profiles — Digital profiles that show who the farmer is, where they farm, what they grow, and how they perform over time.
• Farm-to-market tracking — A clear record of inputs, harvests, and sales so partners can see how crops move through the value chain.
• Bank-ready data — Data that is structured, traceable, and aligned with AML/CFT and KYC standards, so institutions can plug it into their existing risk processes.
```

**Impact:**
- ✂️ Word count: 120 words → 70 words (42% reduction)
- ✅ Removes redundancy with "Who we serve"
- ✅ Lets the bullets do the explaining (they're already great)
- ✅ One punchy sentence = faster comprehension
